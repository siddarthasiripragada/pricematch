# PriceMatch

## Overview

PriceMatch is a grocery flyer intelligence and price-comparison prototype designed to help users compare retail offers across stores and make more informed shopping decisions.

The project focuses on a common consumer workflow: browsing weekly flyers, finding relevant items, comparing prices, and identifying where a shopper can get better value.

## Business Problem

Grocery shoppers often review multiple flyers manually across retailers. This process is time-consuming, difficult to compare consistently, and hard to personalize when users are looking for specific items or weekly deals.

PriceMatch explores how flyer data, item discovery, and comparison workflows can be brought together into a more structured product experience.

## Key Capabilities

- Flyer-oriented retail browsing experience
- Grocery price comparison workflow
- Search-focused item discovery
- Product-style interface for weekly offers
- Foundation for future real flyer ingestion, extraction, and price intelligence

## Technical Focus

- Retail data product design
- Search and comparison workflows
- Front-end application prototyping
